Fault Lab

Inject realistic sensor and motion faults to study how they distort the map. These affect virtual scans and movement, not the room geometry itself.
Angle jitter: random per-reading bearing error (simulates mounting wobble).
Heading offset/drift: reported heading differs from true heading (simulates compass calibration error / gyro drift).
Wheel slip: actual movement is less than commanded (simulates slipping wheels during auto-explore).
Cone width: averages several rays across a beam width (simulates a wide ultrasonic cone).

Lazy LiDAR / Patient LiDAR Protocol Release: v0.4-review (2026-06-12) Review-ready dashboard exhibit release. Includes scroll-safe layout, Fault Lab, scenario JSON save/load, Atlas edition: virtual UART and local CSV replay only. No Bluetooth connection or robot control. Core scan lines: SCAN_BEGIN,<run>,<step_deg>,<samples> SCAN,<relative_angle_deg>,<distance_cm>,<samples>,<quality_0_100>,<timestamp_ms> SCAN_DONE,<run> GSA-style semantic lines: GSA,HELLO,device=LazyLiDAR,role=VirtualRobot GSA,OBSERVE,heading=<absolute_bearing_deg>,distance_cm=<distance>,quality=<q> GSA,SUMMARY,points=<n>,nearest_cm=<d>,farthest_bearing=<bearing> Design principle: The public Atlas edition runs virtually and can replay a local CSV file. Replay does not establish the file’s provenance.
