What does the trace measure?

For each sample, subtract the mean resting X, Y and Z values from the three readings, then take the length of the difference vector. The result is in milli-g. It can change because the robot accelerated, vibrated or tilted. It does not measure speed or distance, and a small signal does not prove the car was stationary.
