How to re-enter the idea years later

1. Picture the water clock.
Water crosses a boundary every second. That is rate.
2. Picture the beaker on the scale.
The collected mass remembers the preceding flow. That is accumulation.
3. Picture the ball on the ramp.
Constant-ish acceleration grows velocity; velocity grows position.
4. Now ask the two calculus questions.
How fast is it changing now? How much has accumulated?
  • Integral: rate → accumulated state.
  • Derivative: state history → local rate.
  • Fundamental Theorem: under appropriate conditions, these two viewpoints fit together.
