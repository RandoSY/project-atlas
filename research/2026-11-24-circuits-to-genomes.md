## Documented V7 results

- 16,384 multiplication verification checks and 540 exhaustive network comparisons across 4,096 input states reported without discrepancies.
- 30 seeded network portfolios: at 32 outputs, reuse saves 50.9% of distinct nodes; mean motif-fault impact rises from 0.96 to 4.08 outputs; targeted buffering reduces it to 0.10 (0.485 under broader internal-node faults).
- At 64 PDP-8 tasks, shared subroutines save 70.8% of code words at 4.1% modeled memory-cycle cost; routine-fault impact rises from 0.93 to 55.82 tasks.
- K562 exploratory genomic pilot: 695 original intervals, 694 uniquely mapped, 693 fully covered, 636 complete cases across 408 genes.
- Adjusted reach coefficient −0.026 (95% CI −0.094 to 0.042); interaction 0.012 (−0.056 to 0.080). Held-out chromosome MSE: 0.4332 baseline, 0.4356 reach, 0.4390 full model.
- Within-gene buffering coefficient −0.398 (p=0.046) reverses to +0.704 (p=0.129) when focal raw ABC numerator enters the model.
- H2 not supported as operationalized. H3 and H3-M (Functional Equivalence Mapping) remain prospective, not validated. A null association does not establish equivalence or prove turnover.

Source: V7 manuscript and manifest in the canonical research repository; results reported, not independently rerun for this checkpoint.
