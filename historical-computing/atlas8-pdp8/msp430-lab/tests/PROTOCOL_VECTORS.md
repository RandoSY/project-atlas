# ATLAS-LAB/1 golden vectors

CRC: CCITT-FALSE, initialization `0xFFFF`, polynomial `0x1021`, high byte first; `"123456789"` produces `0x29B1`. Each frame begins `A5`, then version `01`, sequence, opcode, length, payload, CRC high, CRC low.

| Operation | Raw bytes, hexadecimal |
|---|---|
| IDENTIFY, seq 01 | `A5 01 01 01 00 F6 75` |
| ADC_START A0, seq 01 | `A5 01 01 10 01 00 BD BB` |
| STATUS, seq 01 | `A5 01 01 30 00 C0 D1` |

These frames are generated by `lab_protocol.frame`, and should match the output from `lab_pack` in the target firmware before attempting a physical UART connection. Command values and response payloads are defined in [LAB_IO_V070.md](../../docs/LAB_IO_V070.md).
