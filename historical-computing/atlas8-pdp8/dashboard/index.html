HIGH-SPEED PAPER-TAPE READER · DEVICE 01Original DEC RIM boot instructions; CPU executes every IOT

1 · Install authentic RIM loader

Deposits the documented 17 PDP-8 words into 7756–7776. Or toggle the same words manually using the Front Panel.

This step represents console switch entry. The paper tape itself is never decoded by JavaScript.

2 · Mount a paper tape

10 cps simulates ASR-33 timing on device 01; this is not an ASR-33 reader-device implementation. Raw 8-bit frames; maximum 32 KiB.

3 · Operate the real loader

STOP → ENTER RIM → mount tape → start at 7756. The reader fetches actual frames through 6014, 6011 and 6016.

Tape not mounted

The guest executes the original RIM IOTs for every byte, even at TURBO speed. At end-of-tape the loader waits; STOP to examine memory.
