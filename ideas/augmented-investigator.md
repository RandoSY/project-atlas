# The Augmented Investigator

**Status:** incubator / Version 0.1 working manuscript, 24 November 2026. Not peer reviewed.
**Author:** Randall S. Young, Independent Researcher, Indiana, USA; [ORCID](https://orcid.org/0009-0003-1657-0243).

**Problem:** Interdisciplinary investigators may have productive intuitions but lack specialized mathematical, statistical or computational skills needed to investigate them.

**Central idea:** Interactive AI can potentially expand the questions an individual can investigate, not merely speed up writing. The human–AI process is itself an observable research object. The first study investigates nature; the second investigates the investigation.

**Evidence:** The 23 November conceptual manuscript reported no original empirical findings; the 24 November V7 reports computational benchmarks and a negative exploratory genomic pilot. The investigator describes real-time terminal-based assistance. V7 discloses ChatGPT and Google Gemini/Antigravity assistance. These artifacts establish a bounded feasibility case, not causal productivity gains.

**Mechanism proposed:** human question → joint operationalization → implementation → execution → critical evaluation → revised question. Human direction, verification and accountability remain essential.

**Metrics:** question-to-evidence latency, methodological reach, substantive iterations, intellectual agency, error correction/retention of null results, research cost.

**Limits:** no unaided control, incomplete transcript audit, no independent reproduction; retrospective self-study with potential confirmation and selection bias. Do not infer active research time from document timestamps or assign unobserved contributions. Preserve errors and abandoned paths, not just successes.

**Reconstruction:** [V0.1 working manuscript](../research/augmented-investigator/The_Augmented_Investigator_v0.1.md); [trace audit protocol](../research/augmented-investigator/TRACE_AUDIT.md); [checkpoint](../research/2026-11-24-circuits-to-genomes.md); [V7 source](https://github.com/RandoSY/architectural-economy/blob/main/publication/Architectural_Economy_v7.txt).

**Disposition:** active evidence recovery; independent peer review and prospective controlled studies needed before strong performance claims.
