# Circuits to Genomes
**Intellectual checkpoint: 24 November 2026 | Randall S. Young | Active research framework**

## Central question

How do relatively simple components, economically organized and precisely coordinated, produce complex, adaptable and enduring systems?

Circuits to Genomes unifies the estate's physical computing, historical computing, measurement, engineering, education and biological inquiry. The phrase crystallized during the educational trajectory discussion from one-bit logic to complex systems. This is a synthesis, not a claim that electronic and biological mechanisms are equivalent or that the underlying scientific concepts are newly discovered.

## Learning and inquiry trajectory

| Level | Examples | Organizing concepts |
|---|---|---|
| 1. One-Bit Wonders | PIC Black Box, NAND latch, clock, reaction game | State, feedback, timing |
| 2. Physical computing | Arduino UNO, micro:bit v2, ESP32/M5Stack | Sensing, computation, control |
| 3. Computational architecture | RP2040, MSP430, PDP-8, LINC | Instruction semantics, composition, reuse, observability |
| 4. Complex systems | SDL, robotics, distributed instrumentation | Coordination, dependency, robustness |
| 5. Genomic architecture | Noncoding regulatory DNA, regulatory networks | Evolutionary constraint, functional continuity |
| 6. AI-augmented inquiry | Human-directed computational analysis | Methodological reach, scientific agility, verification |

Architectural economy means achieving a required portfolio of functions under finite resources through composition, reuse, modularity and coordination, taking account of resilience and adaptation costs. Its six organizing themes are economy, composition, coordination, emergence, robustness and continuity.

> The enduring property of a complex system may reside not in the permanence of its components, but in the continuity of their coordination.

The historical PDP-8 is an illustrative engineering case: compatible instruction behavior can persist across hardware implementations. Biological developmental system drift offers a functional comparison, not an identical mechanism or proof of the proposed genomic hypotheses.

## Education and experimentation

Proposed comparative laboratories: electronic latch versus regulatory bistability; electronic oscillator versus biological rhythm; controller versus homeostasis; redundant paths versus regulatory buffering; compatible PDP-8 implementation versus functional continuity. Electronic experiments demonstrate organizational principles; independent biological data are required to validate transfer. Follow Capture → Analyze → Publish and link to CORE 10, Nugget, computing observatories and SDL rather than duplicating implementations.

## Guiding principles

- Experience Before Equation — learning.
- Need Before Technology — engineering.
- Inquiry Before Expertise — a proposed inquiry principle: a meaningful question can precede mastery of all implementation techniques; the researcher must still understand and verify methods and assumptions.
