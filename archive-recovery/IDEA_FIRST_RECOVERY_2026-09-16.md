# Idea-First Recovery Batch — 16 September 2026

This checkpoint records the estate-preservation pivot from **maximum artifact count** to **durable reconstruction of ideas plus representative evidence**.

The goal is no longer to copy every variant merely because it exists. The goal is to make sure the important intellectual structures survive with enough source, evidence, provenance and validation boundaries for another person to understand and continue the work.

## Preservation rule

For each important family, prefer this order:

1. canonical idea / why it exists;
2. stable architecture and vocabulary;
3. representative implementation/source;
4. validation/evidence;
5. visual explanation where it carries unique understanding;
6. exact historical package/checksum when recoverable;
7. explicit missing-artifact record when not recoverable.

Protected-system boundaries remain unchanged: IAS implementation/private assurance material and protected debugWIRE/LIGHTENING implementation are not auto-published.

## Estate-wide ideas secured

- **The Sufficiency Principle** — necessary vs sufficient as a stopping rule; stewardship replaces expansion after fundamental capability exists.
- **How Much? / Enough?** — quantify reality, then judge sufficiency against a purpose/threshold.
- **Measurement First** — measurement transforms uncertainty into evidence capable of supporting action.
- **Experience Before Equation** — mathematical formalism arrives after the phenomenon creates the need.
- **Need Before Technology** — instrumentation is introduced because a measurement problem earns it.
- **AI-Visible Laboratory / STEP + SUPER** — AI becomes useful inside an evidentiary loop when it can access the same instrument-derived evidence as the learner while provenance and human authority remain visible.
- **Low Cost as Design Constraint** — cheap apparatus is not merely a budget compromise; it is part of accessibility, inspectability and instructional method.
- **Publication as Handoff** — completion means another person can reconstruct, operate, inspect and adapt the work.
