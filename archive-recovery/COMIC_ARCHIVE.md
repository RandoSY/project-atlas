# Comic & Illustrated-Story Recovery Manifest

**Recovery dates:** 15–16 September 2026  
**Purpose:** preserve the illustrated teaching corpus as a first-class part of the Intellectual Estate.

The comic work is not decoration. These pages often contain the clearest and most compact surviving explanation of a project, experiment, architecture, historical progression, or teaching sequence. Under the estate's **idea-backup** rule, comics are high-value **idea carriers**.

## Preservation method

- Originals remain untouched in the ChatGPT Library.
- Preservation copies are organized under `/Intellectual Estate Recovery/Comics/`.
- Public-safe work goes under `Public/`.
- Material crossing the protected-system boundary is duplicated into `Protected/` and is **not** published to GitHub.
- Generic generated filenames such as `image-gen-N(...)` are grouped by generation timestamp, visually inspected, identified by page title, and renamed as ordered `Page_01.png`, `Page_02.png`, etc.
- Incomplete sets are preserved as-is and missing pages are recorded as forensic targets rather than silently ignored.
- GitHub records the public manifest and conceptual context; large image originals remain byte-preserved in the Library archive unless selective mirroring adds real preservation value.

## Why the comics matter to the idea backup

Several ideas are reconstructed more efficiently from these visual sequences than from a directory of source code:

- **Fidelity Progression / One Phenomenon Many Realizations** visually preserves the representation architecture.
- **Reaction Game — Four Generations** preserves Catch-the-Clock as technological succession around one stable phenomenon.
- **SUPER + MCP + AI** and **Why Build SUPER?** preserve the transition from ordinary instrument to AI-visible laboratory.
- **NOTHING IS FREE** preserves the energy-accounting narrative.
- **Experience Before Equation** captures a core pedagogical rule in one visual artifact.
- **Bargain Bin Robotics** preserves the central 'clever instead of expensive' rationale.
- **Agnes Pockels** visual material turns historical surface science into an experimental lineage rather than a biographical aside.
- **The Quest for Enough** preserves the estate-wide transition from measuring quantity to determining sufficiency against a requirement and a boundary.
- **SDL 8.0 Architecture Overview** preserves the unified multi-runtime architecture: stable MCP verbs above eForth, MicroBlocks and CircuitPython adapters, with deployment separated from runtime YMP communication.

This is why image preservation is not postponed as cosmetic work. The visuals are part of the explanatory source record.

## GitHub note

Many image originals are multi-megabyte files and the available GitHub connector is principally text-oriented. The Library recovery archive is therefore the byte-preserved image repository, while GitHub carries this manifest, conceptual indexes and selectively mirrored artifacts when safe and practical.

A comic is considered preserved for the estate when its ordered original pages are secured, its public/protected disposition is known, and its conceptual relationship to the estate is recoverable—not merely when GitHub's visible file count increases.
