# Comic & Illustrated-Story Recovery Manifest

**Recovery date:** 15 September 2026  
**Purpose:** preserve the illustrated teaching corpus as a first-class part of the Intellectual Estate.

The comic work is not treated as decoration. These pages often contain the clearest surviving explanation of a project, experiment, architecture, or teaching sequence. Recovery therefore preserves complete page sets whenever possible, not isolated images.

## Preservation method

- Originals remain untouched in the ChatGPT Library.
- Preservation copies are organized under `/Intellectual Estate Recovery/Comics/`.
- Public-safe work goes under `Public/`.
- Material crossing the protected-system boundary is duplicated into a protected off-GitHub archive and is **not** enumerated publicly in detail.
- Generic generated filenames such as `image-gen-N(...)` are grouped by generation timestamp, visually inspected, identified by page title, and renamed as ordered `Page_01.png`, `Page_02.png`, etc.
- Incomplete sets are preserved as-is and missing pages are recorded as forensic targets rather than silently ignored.

## Current preservation count

## GitHub note

The image originals are generally much larger than ordinary source files, and the available GitHub connector is primarily text-oriented. For that reason, the Library recovery archive currently holds the byte-preserved images while GitHub holds this public manifest and the project/source context. Public image mirroring can be done selectively where safe and practical without risking loss of the originals.
