## Rule

A fork can still be historically important. Preserve the relationship, local experiments built around it, and any clearly attributable estate-authored modifications. Do not erase upstream authorship by flattening a fork into a canonical estate repository.

This ledger is intentionally expandable as the deep recovery pass classifies older repositories.
