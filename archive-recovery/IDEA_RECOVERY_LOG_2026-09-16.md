# Idea-First Recovery Log — 16 September 2026

This dated log records the point at which the Intellectual Estate recovery was explicitly reframed from **file backup** to **idea backup**.

## Governing change

The estate now treats files, code, manuals, screenshots, comics, datasets and repositories as **evidence containers**. The archival unit of value is the durable idea.

A project is adequately preserved only when a future reader can recover its problem, central idea, significance, mechanism, rationale, distinctive contribution, representative evidence, conceptual evolution, limits and reconstruction path.

This rule is now formalized in:

- `IDEA_PRESERVATION_PRINCIPLE.md`
- `IDEA_INDEX.md`
- `IDEA_CARD_TEMPLATE.md`

Project Atlas README now makes these the front door to the estate.

## High-value synthesis recovered

### Full Measure (Enough)

The synthesis has been preserved as `works-and-publications/Full_Measure_Enough_IDEA_RECORD.md`.

Its durable architecture is:

**How much?** — quantify reality.

**Enough?** — judge sufficiency against a purpose, requirement, threshold, need or constraint.

Operationally:

**OBSERVE → MEASURE → MODEL → DETERMINE THE REQUIREMENT → PROVIDE ENOUGH → STOP**

The key contribution is **measured sufficiency**: neither blind maximization nor vague minimalism, but explicit requirements, uncertainty/margin, evidence and a stopping rule.

### Everything Begins with Measurement

A 21-page measurement-science framework was recovered from the Library. Because the historical source contains protected material, a sanitized public idea record was created at:

`works-and-publications/papers/Everything_Begins_with_Measurement_PUBLIC_IDEA_RECORD.md`

The public framework preserves:

- start with phenomenon and measurand, not board/sensor;
- `phenomenon → sensor → calibration → measurement → communication → record → model → decision → action`;
- measurement quality before statistics/signal analysis before estimation before longitudinal inference;
- project filter: Phenomenon / Measure / Trust / Interpret / Act;
- completion criterion: another person can reproduce the measurement chain, inspect uncertainty and justify the action.

Protected implementation material from the source was intentionally omitted.

### Full Measure — Instrumented Inquiry

A 12-page working paper was recovered and distilled at:

`works-and-publications/papers/Full_Measure_Instrumented_Inquiry_IDEA_RECORD.md`

Its durable sequence is:

**LOOK → MEASURE → NOTICE → CONJECTURE → TEST → INTERPRET**

Sensors are treated as **epistemic amplifiers**—better eyes for quantities—not as authority. Historical scientists and equations should arrive after the learner encounters the question they help answer.

The preserved six-question protocol is:

1. What happened?
2. What can we measure?
3. What pattern is present?
4. What might explain it?
5. What evidence would distinguish the explanations?
6. What did the evidence actually support?

### When the AI Can See the Experiment — STEP + SUPER

An 18-page September 2026 conceptual paper was recovered and distilled at:

`works-and-publications/papers/When_AI_Can_See_the_Experiment_IDEA_RECORD.md`

The central thesis is that AI's laboratory role changes when it has bounded access to the **same instrument-derived evidence** as the learner.

STEP is:

**Sense → Test → Explain → Propose → repeat**

The paper introduces **intellectual branching cost**: the friction required to turn an unanticipated question into a scientifically coherent evidence-producing extension. The proposed benefit of AI is primarily to reduce this cost, not to automate the learner out of inquiry.

The preserved evidence boundary is:

**The AI may interpret the evidence, but it may not manufacture the evidence.**

Measured, simulated, calculated and AI-inferred quantities must remain distinguishable.

## Existing artifact recovery reinterpreted under the idea rule

Earlier deep-recovery work remains valuable, but its purpose is now clearer:

- exact repository snapshots preserve provenance and reconstructability;
- runnable browser labs preserve executable explanations;
- manuals preserve design rationale and operator intent;
- validation records preserve the limits of claims;
- source files preserve representative mechanisms;
- comics and illustrated narratives preserve high-density conceptual explanations;
- historical versions are retained when they show conceptual evolution, not merely because they exist.

## Representative implementation emphasis

Recovery now favors one or a few representative implementations per durable idea rather than mechanically duplicating every version.

Examples:

- validated-v6 65C02 simulator + validation + curriculum represent visible/reversible computing;
- later v8/v9 records preserve the meaningful move toward Forth and Studio/debugging without requiring every intermediate HTML build;
- UNO/MFS v1.2 is preferred over copying v1.0/v1.1 duplicates because it captures standalone-first operation, four-sensor reuse, local/remote ownership, explicit health/failure state and transport-independent ASCII control;
- Catch-the-Clock keeps representative firmware, simulators and teaching material because the important idea is technological succession around one stable phenomenon.

## Protected boundary reaffirmed

IAS / Independence Assurance remains deliberately off GitHub. Mixed historical sources are never treated as permission to publish protected sections. Public derivatives preserve general educational/measurement ideas while omitting protected implementation details.

Detailed DebugWire LIGHTENING implementation remains protected as well.

## Completion criterion after the pivot

The recovery is complete when every durable public idea has enough preserved context and evidence that a capable future reader—or a future reader working with AI—can understand it, test it, teach it, reconstruct it or extend it without the original author present.

**File count is evidence of activity, not the success metric. Survival of meaning is the success metric.**
