# Idea Card Template

Use this for durable concepts, systems, experiments, protocols, and teaching architectures. The goal is to preserve meaning, not merely inventory artifacts.

## Name

Canonical name plus important historical names.

## Problem

What need, question, limitation, frustration, or opportunity caused this idea to exist?

## Central idea

State the contribution in the smallest clear form possible.

## Why it matters

What becomes possible, cheaper, clearer, safer, fairer, more teachable, or more durable because of this idea?

## How it works

Mechanism, architecture, experiment, protocol, mathematical model, workflow, or reasoning chain.

## Why this design

Important constraints, tradeoffs, rejected approaches, platform choices, and rationale.

## Distinctive contribution

What would be easiest to lose if only source files survived?

## Representative evidence

Source, executable artifacts, measurements, validation, manuals, diagrams, comics, demonstrations, or historical snapshots that support or explain the idea.

## Evolution

Meaningful conceptual changes over time. Do not list duplicate versions that add no intellectual content.

## Limits / unfinished work

What has not been validated? What assumptions remain? What should a future builder test next?

## Reconstruction path

Minimum path for a future reader or AI-assisted builder to reproduce, teach, extend, or reinterpret the idea.

## Relationships

Ideas this depends on, extends, contradicts, or helps explain.
